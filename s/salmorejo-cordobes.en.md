# Córdoba-style cold tomato cream

![Córdoba-style cold tomato cream](salmorejo-cordobes.jpg)

*Photo: [P.Lechien](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Salmorejo_cordouan.JPG), [CC0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/), via Wikimedia Commons.*

🍅 Serves **4** · ⏱️ 15 minutes + 1 hour chilling · Difficulty: easy

*En español:* [Salmorejo cordobés](salmorejo-cordobes.es.md)

## Ingredients

- 2¼ lb very ripe Roma tomatoes
- 7 oz dense day-old white bread
- 1 small garlic clove
- ⅔ cup extra-virgin olive oil
- 1 teaspoon salt
- 1 tablespoon sherry vinegar (optional)

**To serve:** 2 chopped hard-boiled eggs, 3 oz diced serrano ham and olive oil.

## Directions

1. **Blend the tomatoes.** Rinse and chop the tomatoes. Blend well and strain to remove skins and seeds.
2. **Bread.** Tear the crustless bread into pieces and soak it in the tomato purée for 10 minutes.
3. **Emulsify.** Add the garlic and salt and blend. With the blender running, drizzle in the oil until you get a thick, orange cream. Add the vinegar if using.
4. **Chill.** Adjust the salt and refrigerate at least 1 hour.
5. **Serve.** Spoon into bowls and top with chopped egg, ham and a drizzle of oil.

## Tips

- **Thicker than gazpacho:** it should hold on a spoon like a cream.
- **Vegetarian:** skip the ham and top with avocado or tuna.
- **Keeps** 2–3 days in the fridge.
