# Serrano ham croquettes

![Serrano ham croquettes](croquetas-de-jamon.jpg)

*Photo: [Tamorlan](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Croquetas-Jam%C3%B3n-Riofr%C3%ADo.jpg), [CC BY 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/), via Wikimedia Commons.*

🧆 Makes **about 30** · ⏱️ 1 hour (+ 4 hours chilling) · Difficulty: medium

*En español:* [Croquetas de jamón](croquetas-de-jamon.es.md)

## Ingredients

- 5 oz serrano ham, very finely diced
- 4 cups whole milk
- 7 tablespoons butter
- 2 tablespoons olive oil
- ¾ cup all-purpose flour
- ½ onion, very finely chopped (optional)
- Nutmeg, salt and pepper
- 2 beaten eggs and breadcrumbs, for coating
- Olive or sunflower oil, for frying

## Directions

1. **Sauté.** In a wide skillet, melt the butter with the oil and cook the onion 5 minutes. Add the ham and sauté 1 minute.
2. **Béchamel.** Add the flour and toast it 2–3 minutes, stirring. Slowly pour in the hot milk, whisking constantly.
3. **Thicken.** Cook over low heat 15–20 minutes, stirring, until the dough pulls away from the pan. Season with salt (carefully, the ham is salty), pepper and nutmeg.
4. **Chill.** Spread the mixture in a dish, press plastic wrap onto the surface and refrigerate at least 4 hours.
5. **Shape and coat.** Scoop portions with two spoons, shape into small logs, dip in egg and then breadcrumbs.
6. **Fry.** Fry in batches in hot oil (350 °F) until golden. Drain on paper towels.

## Tips

- **For creaminess:** the dough should be soft; little flour and a long cook.
- **So they don't burst:** very hot oil, small batches and well-chilled dough.
- **They freeze** well once coated; fry straight from frozen.
