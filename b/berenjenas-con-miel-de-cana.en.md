# Fried eggplant with cane honey

![Fried eggplant with cane honey](berenjenas-con-miel-de-cana.jpg)

*Photo: [Timo Tijhof](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Berenjenas_con_Miel.jpg), [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/), via Wikimedia Commons.*

🍆 Serves **4** · ⏱️ 30 minutes (+ 1 hour soaking) · Difficulty: easy

*En español:* [Berenjenas con miel de caña](berenjenas-con-miel-de-cana.es.md)

## Ingredients

- 2 medium eggplants
- 2 cups milk (or salted water)
- 1¼ cups all-purpose flour
- Olive oil, for frying
- Cane honey or molasses, to taste
- Flaky salt

## Directions

1. **Cut.** Rinse the eggplants and cut into sticks or ½-inch slices, skin on.
2. **Soak.** Soak in the milk (or salted water) for 1 hour; this removes bitterness and helps them absorb less oil.
3. **Dredge.** Drain without fully drying and toss in flour, shaking off the excess.
4. **Fry.** Fry in batches in plenty of very hot oil (350 °F) until golden and crisp. Drain on paper towels.
5. **Serve.** Sprinkle with flaky salt and drizzle with cane honey just before serving.

## Tips

- **For crispness:** fry in small batches so the oil stays hot.
- **Serve right away:** the honey softens the coating, so add it last.
- **No cane honey?** Regular honey, molasses or date syrup also work.
