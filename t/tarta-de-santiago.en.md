# Galician almond cake

![Galician almond cake](tarta-de-santiago.jpg)

*Photo: [Katrin Gilger](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Tarta_de_Santiago_(4467497071).jpg), [CC BY-SA 2.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/2.0/), via Wikimedia Commons.*

🎂 Serves **8** · ⏱️ 50 minutes · Difficulty: easy

*En español:* [Tarta de Santiago](tarta-de-santiago.es.md)

## Ingredients

- 2½ cups (9 oz) almond flour (blanched almonds, finely ground)
- 1¼ cups sugar
- 5 large eggs
- Zest of 1 lemon
- ½ teaspoon ground cinnamon (optional)
- 1 splash brandy or anise liqueur (optional)
- Butter, for the pan
- Powdered sugar, to decorate

## Directions

1. **Prep the pan.** Preheat the oven to 350 °F. Grease a 9-inch round cake pan and line the bottom with parchment paper.
2. **Beat.** Beat the eggs with the sugar until pale and slightly foamy.
3. **Mix.** Fold in the almond flour, lemon zest, cinnamon and liqueur until smooth.
4. **Bake.** Pour into the pan and bake 35–40 minutes, until golden and a toothpick comes out clean.
5. **Decorate.** Let cool and unmold. Place a cross of Saint James stencil in the center and dust with powdered sugar. Lift the stencil off carefully.

## Tips

- **Gluten-free:** the traditional recipe has no flour.
- **Good almonds:** Marcona almonds, or ground with skins for a more rustic flavor.
- **Stencil:** print the cross of Saint James and cut it out of paper or card stock.
